Kaytchi: The Millionaire Wannabe

The True LARP. Napoleons.

At seventeen, Kaytchi finds himself at the beginning of a journey that he hopes will eventually lead him far beyond the circumstances of his youth. While the future remains uncertain, one ambition has already become central to the way he sees his life: the desire to become a millionaire and build something that belongs entirely to him.

For Kaytchi, the idea of wealth extends beyond the possession of money. It represents independence, opportunity, and the ability to shape his own future. He does not simply wish to participate in the world of business; he wants to establish his own place within it. It is an ambition that demands time, patience, and a willingness to work long after the initial excitement of an idea has disappeared.

His days are increasingly occupied by the pursuit of that vision. Through long hours of work, the development of ideas, and the process of turning plans into practical ventures, Kaytchi has begun laying the foundations of a future that remains largely unwritten. He understands that ambition alone cannot guarantee success, yet it continues to serve as the force behind his decisions. At an age when many are still considering the possibilities available to them, he has chosen to begin exploring one of the most uncertain paths: entrepreneurship.

The first expression of this ambition is LEVYE, his clothing brand. More than an entry into the fashion industry, the company represents an initial attempt to transform a personal vision into something tangible. It gives Kaytchi a starting point from which he can learn the realities of building a brand, understanding a market, and managing the responsibilities that come with creating a business of his own. Whether LEVYE will eventually become the cornerstone of a larger enterprise remains to be seen, but its significance lies in the fact that the pursuit has already begun.

Yet Kaytchi's ambitions do not end with clothing. He envisions establishing additional companies in the years ahead, gradually expanding beyond a single industry and developing a collection of ventures under his direction. The scale of this vision remains uncertain, as do the challenges that will accompany it. For now, the future exists as a series of possibilities — opportunities to be pursued, mistakes to be learned from, and decisions that will determine the direction of his journey.

Alongside Raytchi, Kaytchi is associated with a shared designation: “The True LARP. Napoleons.” The phrase carries a peculiar mixture of irony and grandeur. It evokes the image of two individuals who dare to imagine themselves on a scale larger than their present circumstances, while remaining conscious of the distance between the identities they project and the achievements they have yet to attain. The word Napoleons suggests ambition, influence, and the desire to leave a lasting mark. Meanwhile, LARP introduces an element of self-awareness, acknowledging the theatrical nature of an identity that is still in the process of becoming reality.

Perhaps this contradiction is what makes the title distinctive. It does not necessarily claim that success has already been achieved. Instead, it reflects the mindset of individuals who have chosen to pursue a vision before they possess the means to fully realize it. For Kaytchi and Raytchi, the name becomes both an expression of ambition and a reminder that the image of success is only the beginning of a much longer process.

The title The Millionaire Wannabe similarly captures Kaytchi at this particular stage of his life. It describes someone who has not yet reached the destination but has already committed himself to the pursuit. There is an inherent uncertainty in the title, a recognition that the outcome is not guaranteed. It may be interpreted as a declaration of intent, an ironic self-portrait, or simply an honest description of a young man who wants more from his future than what he currently possesses.

Whatever the interpretation, the distinction between aspiration and accomplishment remains essential. A business empire cannot be built on an image alone, and financial success cannot be established through ambition alone. The years ahead will require Kaytchi to turn his ideas into sustainable ventures, make difficult decisions, and discover whether his determination can withstand the realities of the business world.

At seventeen, he still has much to learn. His story is not one of established wealth or an empire already completed, but of an individual standing at the threshold of something he hopes to build. LEVYE is his first step; the companies he imagines remain part of a future yet to come. Between the two lies the work that will ultimately define his progress.

For now, Kaytchi remains a young entrepreneur in the making, driven by a vision that exceeds his present achievements. His ambitions may be grand, his identity deliberately enigmatic, and his destination uncertain. But every journey begins somewhere, and his has begun with a name, a clothing brand, and the determination to see how far he can go.

He is not yet the millionaire he hopes to become. He is Kaytchi — the millionaire wannabe, one of the Napoleons, and the author of a story that has only just begun.
